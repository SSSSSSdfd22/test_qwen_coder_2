Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a photography session? +

Simply fill out the contact form above or email us at bookings@hikariphotography.com. Include your preferred dates, location, and the type of session you're interested in. We'll respond within 24-48 hours to confirm availability and discuss details.

Do you require a deposit? +

Yes, a 30% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking date. The remaining balance is due on the day of the shoot. We accept bank transfers, credit cards, and PayPal.

Can you travel to my location? +

Absolutely! We're based in Kyoto but available for destination shoots worldwide. Travel fees apply for locations more than 50km from our studio. Contact us for a custom quote.
