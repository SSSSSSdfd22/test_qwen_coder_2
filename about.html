About Hikari

The Art of Seeing 美 - Beauty in Every Moment

Our Story

A Journey Through Light

Founded in 2009, Hikari Photography emerged from a deep appreciation for the serene beauty found in everyday moments. The name "Hikari" means "light" in Japanese, reflecting our philosophy that photography is fundamentally about capturing and shaping light to reveal truth and beauty.

Our approach is rooted in Eastern aesthetics - the concept of "ma" (negative space), "wabi-sabi" (finding beauty in imperfection), and "mono no aware" (the pathos of things). These principles guide every shot we take, creating images that resonate with depth and tranquility.

Based in Kyoto but traveling worldwide, we specialize in nature, wildlife, street, and lifestyle photography. Each project is an opportunity to tell a unique story through our distinctive lens.
