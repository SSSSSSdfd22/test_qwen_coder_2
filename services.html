FAQ

Common Questions

How far in advance should I book a session? +

We recommend booking at least 2-4 weeks in advance, especially for weekend sessions. For commercial projects or destination shoots, please contact us 6-8 weeks ahead to ensure availability.

What happens if the weather is bad? +

For outdoor sessions, we monitor weather conditions closely. If conditions are unfavorable, we'll reschedule your session at no additional cost. Your comfort and the quality of images are our priority.

How long until I receive my photos? +

Standard delivery time is 2-3 weeks for most sessions. You'll receive a preview gallery within 48 hours, and the complete edited collection within the timeframe specified in your package.

Do you travel for shoots? +

Yes! We're based in Kyoto but available for destination shoots worldwide. Travel fees vary by location. Contact us to discuss your dream location and we'll create a custom package.

Can I purchase additional edited images? +

Absolutely. Additional edited images are available for $25 each. We also offer bulk discounts for purchases of 10+ additional images.
